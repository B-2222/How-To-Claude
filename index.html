Trickshot Range

An arena trickshot shooter. Phase 1: movement and shooting feel.

Play in the browser

Loads about 35 MB. Click the game to lock the mouse, press Esc to release it. Desktop browser with WebGL 2 required.

Controls

W A S DMove
ShiftSprint
SpaceJump. Hold it to auto-hop on landing
CCrouch, or slide above 6 m/s. Release mid-slide to slide-cancel
QSlow-motion dive
Left mouseFire
Right mouseAim down sights
F3Toggle the help overlay
BackspaceRestart

Use C to slide, not Ctrl. Ctrl+W closes the tab and no page can stop it.

How to build speed

Jump, then in the air hold one strafe key and turn the mouse the same way, smoothly. Do not hold W. Around 240 to 380 degrees per second of turn is the sweet spot. Hold Space the whole time so you re-jump the instant you land.

The speed meter has ticks at walk and sprint speed. Everything right of the second tick you earned by strafing.
